Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026

This page says what this site collects, why it collects it, who touches it, and how to make it go away. It is written to be read, not to be skipped.

The short version

We collect only what is needed to answer your request. We do not sell your information, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not run trackers unless you allow them. Ask us to delete your information and we do it within 30 days.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site. Nothing is gathered in the background.

What we never collect

No form on this site asks for any of the following. If a page ever does, it is not ours.

  • Social Security number or driver license number
  • Date of birth
  • Card numbers, CVV, or bank account details
  • Health information
  • Photos of government identification
  • Precise device location

Automatic information

Our host records standard server logs, which include an IP address, browser type, and the page requested. Those logs exist to keep the site online and to stop abuse. They are held for about 30 days and then rolled off.

Cookies and third parties

This site sets no advertising cookies. A small entry in your browser remembers your privacy choices so we do not ask again on every visit. Maps and anonymous page counts stay switched off until you turn them on.

How long we keep it

  • Requests and quotes. 24 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Email threads. 36 months, then archived or deleted.
  • Server logs. About 30 days, handled by the host.

How it is protected

  • Every page loads over HTTPS. Plain HTTP is redirected and refused.
  • A content security policy limits what code the browser is allowed to run.
  • Forms are filtered for spam and abuse before anything is sent.
  • Admin and lead pages are blocked from search engines and never cached.
  • No API keys or credentials sit in the browser code.

Your rights

Whatever state you are in, we honor all of these.

  • Ask what we have on you
  • Get a copy of it
  • Correct anything wrong
  • Have it deleted
  • Opt out of any marketing message
  • Get a straight answer within 30 days

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child sent us something, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it. Material changes get posted on the site.
